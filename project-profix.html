Overview

Profix Handyman Services provides home repair and property maintenance in Orange County, California. I took their website from the initial design phase to a final, fully working Webflow site, handling every stage myself: all of the pages designed in Figma, the technical build done in Webflow, and every page made responsive so it works across screen sizes.

Design Approach

The site is designed around one goal: turning a visitor into a quote request. The "Get a Free Quote" and call buttons stay within reach throughout, trust points such as licensed and insured professionals and same-day service sit right below the hero, and a project gallery of before-and-after work lets customers see the quality of the work before they get in touch.

How I Built It
01

Designed every page in Figma

I designed all of the site's pages in Figma first, setting the layout, structure, and visual style before any development started. I also built a clickable mobile prototype to test how the experience would work on a phone.

02

Built the site in Webflow

I handled the technical work in Webflow myself, turning the Figma designs into a live site. That covered the page structure, styling, navigation, the services slider, and the project gallery.

03

Made every page responsive

I checked and adjusted each page so it works across screen sizes, from desktop down to mobile, keeping the quote and call buttons easy to reach wherever the visitor is.

04

Delivered a complete, working site

The result is a finished website, taken from the first design to the final build with no handoff in between, so customers can request a free quote or call in a couple of taps.
